About

I work at the intersection of computer vision, data quality and LLM applications. At Track3D I train SAM2 models that mask construction-site categories and stages, which supports change detection between site captures. A big part of my work is bad-case analysis: finding why a model fails, fixing the labels or data, and checking that the result improved.

Outside work I build LLM apps with evaluation and reliability in mind, and deploy them on Streamlit. I am looking for ML Engineer, Computer Vision or Generative AI roles in Hyderabad, and I am open to remote.
